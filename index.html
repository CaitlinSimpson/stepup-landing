The problem behind StepUp

Built around a specific preparation challenge.

StepUp is designed for Irish public-sector employees preparing for internal promotion through competency-based recruitment.

Who would use it?

Individual employees preparing for promotion interviews. They may use the tool independently.

Who might pay?

Individual users could pay for optional premium features. Organisations may also consider providing access through their Learning and Development departments. Both are proposed business models, not established arrangements.

The problem, in their words

"I have years of experience, but I struggle to identify my strongest examples and explain how they meet the competencies for the next grade."

Illustrative customer statement, not a verified interview quotation.

What the solution must do

Six practical requirements.

These requirements come from the problem identified in the project research.

Enter target-role competencies and capabilities.
Record, edit and save achievements.
Map achievements and highlight evidence gaps.
Structure answers using the STAR method.
Practise questions, receive feedback and retry.
Work on a phone and retain records after refresh.

The demo illustrates these requirements using fictional sample achievements and browser-based storage.

The proposed solution

An evidence planner and interview practice website.

StepUp combines role requirements, achievement records, evidence mapping, STAR preparation and interview practice.

StepUp's proposed difference

StepUp brings achievement recording, competency mapping, evidence-gap identification, STAR preparation and interview practice into one preparation journey.

Business model

How StepUp could be sustained.

The proposed approach is freemium: basic evidence planning and STAR preparation could remain free, with optional paid features in a future version.

An initial pricing assumption is €5 per month for a premium subscription. This is not a validated market price or an active subscription.

Organisational access through Learning and Development departments is another possibility. Hosting, maintenance and any future AI services would create ongoing costs.

Current prototype versus future plans

Available now: evidence bank, competency mapping, STAR builder, interview practice, rule-based feedback and browser-local saving.

Planned only: presentation preparation, subscriptions, organisational accounts, AI-powered feedback and automatic cross-device synchronisation.

A transparent prototype

Clear about what works.

StepUp is a student-built prototype, not an established recruitment service.

Fictional sample data

The demo starts with 10 fictional achievements to demonstrate the tools.

Rule-based feedback

Feedback is based on text checks and structure, not AI evaluation or interview predictions.

Browser-based saving

Records are saved in the current browser. There is no automatic synchronisation between your phone and laptop.
